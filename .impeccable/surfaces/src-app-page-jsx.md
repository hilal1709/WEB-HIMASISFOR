## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is one illustrated journey through Gresik's limestone country, guided by Elvo, where every program fact is a stop on the road; it refuses the category default of a campus-photo hero over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Flat layered vector landscape — limestone grey-white cliffs, deep sea blue, muted pine green, UISI red as the one built-world colour (silos stripes, signposts, buttons), gold only on Elvo and the PMB action. Solid fills, depth by overlap, outlines navy. Rounded pill controls, wooden-signpost and speech-bubble components, a bottom pill nav with Elvo's head at its centre.

STORY: A visitor meets Elvo, learns the program's claim and accreditation, walks the signposted path into four expertise areas, hears alumni and achievements, passes the news board, and ends at the campus gate with "Daftar Sekarang" — while current students jump straight to Akademik/Layanan from the bottom nav.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed illustrated panorama (cliffs left, sea centre, cement plant right). Top-left logo; top-right quiet tagline. Left column headline "Menghubungkan Bisnis & Sistem Informasi untuk Masa Depan" (red second line), accreditation line, red "Daftar Sekarang" + outline "Lihat Kurikulum".
